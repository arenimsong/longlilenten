Home to approximately 500 tightly-knit households, fostering strong bonds, cultural preservation, and collective neighborhood growth.

🌊 Milak River

Gracefully bordered by the pristine Milak River, offering scenic beauty, natural calm, and rich ecological value to our surroundings.

Host to the local PWD office facility, serving as a key administrative hub for infrastructure development in and around Tuli.

A spiritual anchor for our community where residents gather for worship, fellowship, unity, and communal moral guidance.

🌿 Rubber Farms

Picturesque green rubber plantations contributing significantly to the local agrarian economy and sustainable livelihood.

A peaceful communal shelter and rest house providing hospitality and lodging for visitors, travelers, and guests.
